Our story

My name is Kendy, and my family is from southern Haiti. Our family home was damaged, and we are trying to repair it so that we can have a safer place to live, especially when it rains.

My mother also went through a difficult period of illness and hospitalization. The medical expenses used much of the savings our family had set aside for repairing the house.

We are asking kind people around the world to consider supporting us. Every contribution, no matter the amount, can help us move forward. We are grateful for your kindness, prayers, and support.
